# Direct East Face (DEF) Rules & Standards

## 1. Overview & Timing
The Direct East Face of the First Flatiron is Boulder's most iconic scramble. 
The clock starts the moment the athlete passes the **[Chautauqua Trailhead Sign](#cp-start)** and stops the moment they touch the **[First Flatiron Summit Block](#cp-finish)**.

## 2. Required Navigation
* The approach is fully freestyle. You may take any trail network (Chautauqua Trail, Bluebell-Baird, etc.) from the trailhead to the base of the First Flatiron within the **[Approach Corridor](#zone-approach)**.
* The climb itself must stay on the East Face.

## 3. Category Constraints
* **Unsupported**: No rope or technical gear permitted. The ascent and the downclimb must be done unroped. No pacers or partners can assist or hand off gear/water.
* **Supported**: A rope is carried and/or used to rappel the descent off the back.
* **Companionship**: Running or scrambling with partners is allowed, provided the strict no-assistance rule is followed for the Unsupported category.
